Part A • 20 Marks

Modelling, Controllability & Observability

Mathematical derivation of the 6-joint double integrator state-space model, exact zero-order-hold discretization, and rank proofs.

Continuous & Discrete Formulation

The UR3 arm dynamics are linearized via inverse dynamics feedforward into 6 independent double integrators driven by commanded acceleration $\mathbf{u} = \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_{cmd}$:

$$\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{q} \\ \dot{\mathbf{q}} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{12}, \quad \mathbf{u} = \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_{cmd} \in \mathbb{R}^6, \quad \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{q}_{enc} \in \mathbb{R}^6$$ $$\dot{\mathbf{x}}(t) = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{0}_6 & \mathbf{I}_6 \\ \mathbf{0}_6 & \mathbf{0}_6 \end{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}(t) + \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{0}_6 \\ \mathbf{I}_6 \end{bmatrix} \mathbf{u}(t), \quad \mathbf{y}(t) = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{I}_6 & \mathbf{0}_6 \end{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}(t)$$

Under exact zero-order hold ($T_s = 0.010\text{ s}$, $100\text{ Hz}$):

$$\mathbf{A}_d = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{I}_6 & 0.01\mathbf{I}_6 \\ \mathbf{0}_6 & \mathbf{I}_6 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{B}_d = \begin{bmatrix} 5\times 10^{-5}\mathbf{I}_6 \\ 0.01\mathbf{I}_6 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{C} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{I}_6 & \mathbf{0}_6 \end{bmatrix}$$
Controllability & Observability Analysis

Evaluated analytically and verified numerically in joint_model.py:

MatrixEquationRankStatus
Controllability $\mathcal{C} = [\mathbf{B}_d, \mathbf{A}_d\mathbf{B}_d, \dots, \mathbf{A}_d^{11}\mathbf{B}_d]$ 12 / 12 ✓ FULLY CONTROLLABLE

Part B • 20 Marks

Sensing, Calibration & Frame Transformations

Coordinate frame tree, 3D point cloud back-projection, SVD surface normal consistency, and noise suppression.

Coordinate Frame Hierarchy

Rigid body transformations map camera rays into manipulator base space:

{World: W} ──► {Base: B} ──(FK: q)──► {Flange: E} ──► {Camera: C} ──► {Target: O} │ └──► {Tool Pinch: P} (TCP +195mm)
$$\mathbf{p}_{base} = \mathbf{T}_{base}^{flange}(\mathbf{q}) \cdot \mathbf{T}_{flange}^{cam} \cdot \mathbf{T}_{cam}^{opt} \cdot \begin{bmatrix} \frac{(u - c_x)z}{f_x} & \frac{(v - c_y)z}{f_y} & z & 1 \end{bmatrix}^T$$
Computer Vision & Stability Filtering
  • HSV Segmentation: Color masks with morphological opening ($3\times 3$) to eliminate camera noise spikes.
  • Top-Face Isolation: Slices points in the 90th percentile of depth; fits plane normal vector $\mathbf{n}$ using SVD to confirm tabletop horizontal alignment.
  • 4-Fold Cube Symmetry: Wraps yaw angle modulo $\pi/2$ so the robot never executes unnecessary wrist spins: $$\theta_{sym} = \left(\theta + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \bmod \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi}{4}$$
  • Multi-Frame Lock: Requires 10 consecutive frames meeting variance threshold ($< 2\text{ mm}$ position, $< 5^\circ$ yaw) before locking target coordinates.
Part C • 20 Marks

State Estimation & Kalman Filter Observer

6-channel discrete Kalman Filter estimating positions and velocities from noisy encoders ($0.20^\circ$ noise) with dynamic covariance envelopes.

Filter Formulation & Tuning Matrices

Decoupled 2-state discrete Kalman filters per joint with Joseph form covariance stabilization:

$$\mathbf{Q} = \sigma_a^2 \mathbf{B}_d \mathbf{B}_d^T = (2.0)^2 \begin{bmatrix} 0.25 T_s^4 & 0.5 T_s^3 \\ 0.5 T_s^3 & T_s^2 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \sigma_a = 2.0\text{ rad/s}^2$$ $$\mathbf{R} = \sigma_{enc}^2 = (\text{deg2rad}(0.20^\circ))^2 \approx 1.218 \times 10^{-5}\text{ rad}^2$$ $$\mathbf{P}_{k|k} = (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{K}_k \mathbf{C}) \mathbf{P}_{k|k-1} (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{K}_k \mathbf{C})^T + \mathbf{K}_k \mathbf{R} \mathbf{K}_k^T$$

Part D • 20 Marks

Controller Design, Tuning & Tracking

Computed-Torque LQI state feedback with anti-windup clamping and 6-DOF Position-Based Visual Servoing (PBVS).

Computed-Torque LQI Regulator

Combines full inverse dynamics compensation with optimal feedback on state error $e = \hat{x} - x_{des}$:

$$\boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{M}(\hat{\mathbf{q}})\ddot{\mathbf{q}}_{cmd} + \mathbf{c}(\hat{\mathbf{q}}, \dot{\hat{\mathbf{q}}}) - \boldsymbol{\tau}_{passive}$$ $$\ddot{\mathbf{q}}_{cmd} = \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_{des} - \mathbf{K}_q (\hat{\mathbf{q}} - \mathbf{q}_{des}) - \mathbf{K}_v (\dot{\hat{\mathbf{q}}} - \dot{\mathbf{q}}_{des}) - \mathbf{K}_i \int (\hat{\mathbf{q}} - \mathbf{q}_{des}) dt$$

Optimal Gains (Bryson's Rule): $\mathbf{K}_q = 29.3648$, $\mathbf{K}_v = 8.5409$, $\mathbf{K}_i = 21.9352$.
Stability Proof: Discrete closed-loop pole maximum magnitude $|\lambda| = 0.989901 < 1.00$ (strictly stable).

6-DOF Position-Based Visual Servoing

Closed-loop task-space velocity regulation with adaptive Damped Least Squares (DLS):

$$\mathbf{v}_{task} = \begin{bmatrix} K_p (\mathbf{p}_{target} - \mathbf{p}_{tcp}) \\ K_r \text{rotvec}(\mathbf{R}_{target} \mathbf{R}_{tcp}^T) \end{bmatrix}, \quad \dot{\mathbf{q}} = \mathbf{J}^T (\mathbf{J}\mathbf{J}^T + \lambda^2 \mathbf{I})^{-1} \mathbf{v}_{task}$$
  • $K_p = 1.20$, $K_r = 1.00$ ensures smooth exponential error decay without overshoot.
  • DLS damping $\lambda$ dynamically engages when $\sigma_{min}(\mathbf{J}) < 0.05$ to prevent singularity joint rate blowup.
Part E • 20 Marks

Integration & Reactive Safety Supervisor

Dynamic obstacle tracking via overhead 3D Kalman Filter, layered collision avoidance, and payload fail-safe carry hold.

1. Standoff Zone (0.22m)

When an intruder sphere is detected, the avoidance controller computes repulsive velocity vectors to guide the arm around the intruder while continuing the task.

2. Safety Stop (< 0.06m)

If the obstacle penetrates within $60\text{ mm}$ of the robot arm or pinch site, motion is immediately halted (SAFETY STOP) until clearance is verified.

3. Cube Carry Hold & Resume

If the robot is carrying a cube, CubeCarrySafetySupervisor freezes joint poses to prevent dropping. Once cleared, it calculates a smooth return trajectory back to the interrupted task.

Trade-Off: Estimator Bandwidth vs Noise

Scaling Measurement Noise $R$:

  • Scaling $R$ by $0.1\times$ increases filter bandwidth and responsiveness but feeds encoder quantization ripple into computed torque derivatives, causing actuator chatter.
  • Scaling $R$ by $10\times$ provides ultra-smooth torque commands but introduces a $12\text{ ms}$ phase lag during sharp trajectory reversals.
Trade-Off: Control Effort vs Convergence

Scaling Visual Servoing Gain $K_p$:

  • Higher gains ($K_p > 1.8$) reduce convergence time from $2.4\text{ s}$ to $1.2\text{ s}$, but risk overshoot and gripper collision near the table surface.
  • $K_p = 1.20$ guarantees strictly overdamped, monotonic descent without overshoot.
