main.py	
    Reads terminal arguments such as --seed 7, checks the compiled model exists, and starts the simulation.

simulation.py	
    Loads the model, randomises cube and purple positions, runs physics and joint control, displays RGB/depth images, processes camera measurements and saves PBVS logs.

robot_utils.py	
    Shared helpers for finding model elements, reading joint limits and solving IK. This avoids repeating the same code in different files.

observation.py	
    Moves the camera through the zigzag search and holds observation poses so vision can collect measurements.

vision.py	
    Detects colours, converts depth pixels into 3D points, transforms them into robot coordinates, estimates cube sizes and surface poses, and checks measurement stability.

pbvs.py	    
    Calculates movement corrections from fresh camera measurements. It handles pickup approach/descent, transport centring and placement alignment.

stacking.py	    
    Runs the overall sequence: largest cube first, reacquire it, pick it up, lift inward, transport, align, lower, release and repeat. It controls the gripper and trajectory movements.

compile_scene.py
    Builds models/ur3_complete.xml from your source models and scene, adding the movable cubes and purple marker. Run it when the source scene or model changes.
