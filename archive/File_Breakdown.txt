main.py	
    Reads arguments such as --seed 7, checks the compiled model exists, and starts the simulation.
simulation.py	
    Loads the scene, randomises cube and purple positions, runs physics and joint control, displays RGB/depth images, processes measurements, and saves PBVS and observer logs.
robot_utils.py	
    Provides shared helpers for finding model elements, reading joint limits and solving IK.
observation.py
    Runs the zigzag camera search and holds observation poses while vision collects stable measurements.
vision.py	
    Detects colours, converts RGB-D measurements into robot-base coordinates, measures cube sizes and surface poses, and checks measurement stability.
pbvs.py	
    Calculates camera-based movement corrections for pickup approach/descent, transport and placement alignment. Applies speed limits and checks convergence.
stacking.py
    Runs the largest-to-smallest stacking sequence, controls the gripper and trajectories, smooths placement commands, and retries pickup approach after measurement loss.
estimation.py
    Adds simulated encoder noise, estimates joint position and velocity, and logs estimation errors. Currently monitors performance; its estimates do not yet drive control.
compile_scene.py
    Builds models/ur3_complete.xml from the source models and scene. Run it after changing source XML, geometry, mounts or scene definitions.
